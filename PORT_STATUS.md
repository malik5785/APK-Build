# CITY FREEDOM — MAKKAH: Godot 3.6 GLES2 3D Port

Original V10 Godot 4 project is the reference. The confirmed working Godot 3.6 GLES2 diagnostic is the target runtime.

The full game has not been converted. Required conversion areas: 3D geometry batching, character and vehicle physics, Makkah OSM geometry, cameras, traffic, NPCs, audio, UI, and Android TV input.

Do not ship a simplified top-down prototype as the complete game.
